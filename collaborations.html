Collaborations

Better together, always

CareBridge is built to work alongside others. If you have a community that needs support, a platform that can carry a message, or a team looking for somewhere meaningful to put its energy — there is a way to do this together.

Partner Type

Schools & Student Bodies

Run a CareBridge initiative with your student council or club, or open a volunteer intake for your school community.

Partner Type

Communities & Foundations

Tell us what your community needs and we will design a programme around it rather than arriving with one already written.

Partner Type

Organisations & Brands

Sponsor a cycle, supply what an initiative needs, or bring your team along for a day on the ground.

Partner Type

Creators & Media

Help a campaign travel further. Reach is not vanity — it is how the next volunteer finds us.

What a Collaboration Looks Like

  • A conversation first. We start by understanding what you want to achieve, and say honestly whether we are the right fit.
  • A written plan. Scope, dates, responsibilities and cost, agreed before anything is announced.
  • Shared credit. Your name sits alongside ours in every announcement and in the closing report.
  • An honest wrap-up. You receive the same account of what worked and what did not that we publish for ourselves.

What We Ask

Only that the people we serve come first. We do not run initiatives whose primary purpose is publicity, and we will say so early rather than late.

Bring us the need. We will help build the bridge.
