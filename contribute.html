Where Contributions Go

Everything given to CareBridge goes into running initiatives — materials, transport, and the practical costs of getting volunteers and supplies to the people who need them.

Each programme cycle closes with a public report covering what was received, what it was spent on, and what it achieved. If you contribute, you will be able to trace it.

Give what you have. It is always more useful than you think.
